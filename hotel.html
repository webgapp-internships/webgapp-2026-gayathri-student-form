About Us

Royal Stay Hotel is a modern luxury hotel developed as an internship project. Our mission is to provide comfortable accommodation, premium hospitality, online room booking, customer support and efficient hotel management services.

We offer well-furnished rooms, restaurant services, conference halls, travel assistance, laundry service, fitness facilities and entertainment options for guests.

This project demonstrates room management, booking management, customer feedback, room availability and service management using HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Bootstrap.

Our vision is to deliver a memorable guest experience through innovation, comfort and quality service.
