About Our College

Sri Kaliswari College is one of the leading autonomous arts and science colleges located in Sivakasi, Tamil Nadu. The college was established in the year 2000 with the aim of providing quality higher education to students from both rural and urban areas.

The college offers excellent academic facilities with experienced faculty members, modern laboratories, smart classrooms, library facilities, sports activities, placement training, and cultural programs. The institution focuses on discipline, leadership, communication skills, and overall personality development of students.

Sri Kaliswari College encourages students to achieve academic excellence and develop technical skills for successful careers. The college also conducts seminars, workshops, internships, industrial visits, and placement programs for students’ future growth.
